la Uncus more Zelda: ictus uncum catenatum emittit, qui superficiebus aptis vel hostibus haeret teque attrahit; iterum feri ut dimittas

@ Create two linked portals on flat walls. Fire places the first portal; Alt Fire places the second. Walk through either portal to emerge from the other. Your momentum carries through. Assign a weapon slot below: pressing that slot repeatedly cycles its ordinary weapon and every enabled add-on assigned there. Portal view quality is configurable below.
la Duas portas coniunctas in parietibus planis crea. Ictus primam portam ponit; Ictus alter secundam. Per alterutram portam ambula ut ex altera exeas. Impetus tuus transit. Infra locum teli assigna: eundem locum iterum premendo inter telum eius solitum et omnia additamenta activa ibi assignata alternas. Qualitas aspectus portarum infra configurari potest.

@ Hold Fire and sweep the crosshair over monsters to queue targets. Release Fire to throw: the boomerang visits each target, stuns it and returns to you. Alt Fire clears the queue. The weapon slot selects a group, not a separate hotkey: press it again to cycle through the weapons in that slot. Configure stun duration and flight speed below.
la Ictum tene et reticulum super monstra duc ut scopos in ordinem redigas. Ictum dimitte ut iacias: boomerang quemque scopum petit, stupefacit et ad te redit. Ictus alter ordinem purgat. Locus teli turmam eligit, non clavem propriam: iterum preme ut per tela eius loci alternes. Durationem stuporis et celeritatem volatus infra configura.

@ Hold Fire to charge a sticky bomb using rocket ammunition; release to launch. A longer charge throws it faster. Bombs cling to walls, doors and monsters. Alt Fire detonates your planted bombs together. Place a trap, retreat, then detonate it when enemies approach. Assign any weapon slot below; repeated presses cycle through the ordinary weapon and add-ons in that slot.
la Ictum tene ut bombam glutinosam munitione rochetarum ones; dimitte ut emittas. Onus longius eam celerius iacit. Bombae parietibus, ianuis et monstris haerent. Ictus alter bombas positas simul accendit. Insidias pone, recede, deinde accende cum hostes appropinquant. Infra quemvis locum teli assigna; iterum premendo inter telum solitum et additamenta eius loci alternas.

@ Fire launches a chain hook. It latches onto a permitted surface or enemy and reels you toward the anchor. Fire again or use Alt Fire to let go. Choose which materials and enemies can be hooked in Configure Parameters. Assign it to any weapon slot; repeated presses of the slot's existing binding cycle through its weapons.
la Ictus uncum catenatum emittit. Superficiei permissae vel hosti haeret teque ad ancoram attrahit. Iterum feri vel Ictum alterum adhibe ut dimittas. Quae materiae et qui hostes unco capi possint in Parametros configurare elige. Cuivis loco teli assigna; clavem eius loci iterum premendo per tela eius alternas.

@ A piercing railgun inspired by Quake III. Fire sends a slug through every enemy along its line. Each shot consumes two cells, with a 1.5 second refire interval. Damage and the spiral trail colour are configurable below. Choose a weapon slot: the slot's current binding selects and cycles the weapons assigned to it.
la Railgun transfigens a Quake III inspiratum. Ictus glandem per omnem hostem in linea sua mittit. Quisque ictus duas cellas consumit, cum intervallo 1,5 secundarum. Damnum et color vestigii spiralis infra configurari possunt. Locum teli elige: clavis eius loci tela ei assignata eligit et alternat.

@ A Portal-style gel sprayer. Fire sprays blue Repulsion gel: land, jump or run into it to bounce, and landings on it never hurt. Alt Fire sprays orange Propulsion gel: run across it to go much faster. Gel coats floors and walls until washed off with the Powerwasher. Assign a weapon slot below; repeated presses cycle the weapons in that slot.
la Aspersorium gelatinae more Portal. Ictus gelatinam caeruleam Repulsionis spargit: in eam descende, sali vel curre ut resilias, et descensus in eam numquam nocent. Ictus alter gelatinam aurantiam Propulsionis spargit: per eam curre ut multo celerius eas. Gelatina pavimenta et parietes tegit donec Lavatore pressorio abluatur. Infra locum teli assigna; iterum premendo per tela eius loci alternas.

@ A water cannon. Fire shoots a straight jet with limited reach; Alt Fire a wide, shorter fan. The water pushes enemies back, turns projectiles aside and washes away gel and blood. Push strength is configurable below. Assign a weapon slot below; repeated presses cycle the weapons in that slot.
la Tormentum aquarium. Ictus iactum rectum spatio limitato emittit; Ictus alter flabellum latum et brevius. Aqua hostes repellit, proiectilia avertit et gelatinam sanguinemque abluit. Vis impulsus infra configurari potest. Infra locum teli assigna; iterum premendo per tela eius loci alternas.

@ One equipment add-on includes both night vision and thermal vision. The Change Goggles action cycles Normal, Night Vision, Thermal, then Normal again. Night vision brightens dark rooms; thermal vision highlights living enemies. Goggles do not occupy a weapon slot. Configure intensity and sensor noise below. The binding affects only this add-on.
la Unum additamentum instrumenti visionem nocturnam et thermicam continet. Actio Perspicilla mutare alternat Solitam, Nocturnam, Thermicam, deinde iterum Solitam. Visio nocturna conclavia obscura illustrat; visio thermica hostes vivos extollit. Perspicilla locum teli non occupant. Vim et strepitum sensorii infra configura. Assignatio hoc tantum additamentum afficit.

@ Press Dodge with a movement direction on the ground or in midair: dive forward or leap backward and sideways. Diagonals are supported. Each press launches once, with a short recovery. The player body animates in third person. Assign the Dodge action below or in Controls.
la Declinationem preme cum directione motus in solo vel in aere: prorsus te praecipita vel retro et in latus sali. Directiones obliquae sustinentur. Quaeque pressio semel emittit, cum brevi recuperatione. Corpus lusoris in tertia persona movetur. Actionem Declinatio infra vel in Moderaminibus assigna.

@ Use the dedicated Bullet Time action to slow the whole local game to 20% speed. Hold is the default; Toggle switches on each press. la Actione propria Tempus lentum adhibe ut totum lusum localem ad celeritatem 20% retardes. Tenere praefinitum est; Alternare quaque pressione mutat. la Ambo sclopeta et milites hostiles globulos physicos cum dispersione et damno originalibus iaciunt. la Lusum per momentum congelat cum hostem laedis, ut ictus graviores videantur. Lusor singularis tantum.
