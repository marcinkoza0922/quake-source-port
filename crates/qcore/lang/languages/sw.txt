@ Hold Fire and sweep the crosshair over monsters to queue targets. Release Fire to throw: the boomerang visits each target, stuns it and returns to you. Alt Fire clears the queue. @ Hold Fire to charge a sticky bomb using rocket ammunition; release to launch. A longer charge throws it faster. Bombs cling to walls, doors and monsters. sw Shikilia Fyatua kuchaji bomu linalonata kwa kutumia risasi za roketi; achilia kulirusha. Kuchaji kwa muda mrefu hulirusha kwa kasi zaidi. Mabomu hushikamana na kuta, milango na mazimwi. @ Fire launches a chain hook. It latches onto a permitted surface or enemy and reels you toward the anchor. Fire again or use Alt Fire to let go. @ A Portal-style gel sprayer. Fire sprays blue Repulsion gel: land, jump or run into it to bounce, and landings on it never hurt.
